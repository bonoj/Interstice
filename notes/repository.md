# Repository

A note about an executable reality currently being built.

Repository is not itself a place its inhabitants must enter. It contains contexts in which entities can exist, meet, change, and carry consequences elsewhere.

> **Repository owns identity. Places provide context. Systems provide behavior. Relationships connect entities. Consequences survive crossings.**

A place may matter enormously without defining what an entity is.

A moth incubated in Meadow is not a `MeadowMoth`. A floater made in Workshop is not a `WorkshopFloater`. Meadow and Workshop can give those entities capabilities, history, structure, habits, damage, memory, or other consequences without trapping them there.

So a moth from Meadow can meet a floater from Workshop elsewhere in Repository. It can pilot the floater. They can enter a Crucible together. The floater can leave damaged. The moth can leave remembering what happened. Workshop can later encounter that same damaged floater; Meadow can later encounter that same moth with that experience.

The useful direction is therefore not a pipeline such as:

`Workshop -> Betwixt -> Meadow`

Consequence can ripple through the whole executable reality.

Places supply context. Systems act where their requirements are present. Relationships may connect entities across those contexts. Identity persists unless executable evidence eventually teaches us something better.

This is a note, not a specification. Repository's executable evidence has authority over it.
